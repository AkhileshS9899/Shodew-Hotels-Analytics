# Shadow Hotels – Data Analytics Dashboard

---

## Executive Summary

Using *SQL, Excel, and Power BI*, I analyzed Shadow Hotels’ booking and revenue data to provide actionable insights. After identifying trends in revenue, occupancy, cancellations, and booking platforms, I recommend management focus on high-performing properties, optimize underutilized rooms, and improve cancellation handling:

1. Optimize pricing and promotions for low-occupancy weekdays.  
2. Focus on premium room categories to reduce cancellations.  
3. Evaluate booking platforms to balance revenue and cancellation risk.

---

## Business Problem

Shadow Hotels, part of the Shodwe Group with properties across India, faced challenges due to fragmented, Excel-based reporting:  

- Reports were manual and inconsistent, leading to unreliable KPIs.  
- No real-time visibility into occupancy and revenue.  
- Limited centralized view across properties, making comparisons difficult.  
- Weekend vs. weekday trends were not tracked, impacting pricing strategy.  
- Insights into room category performance and cancellations were limited.

## Methodology

1. Combined and cleaned multiple datasets (*fact_bookings, fact_aggregated_bookings, dim_hotels, dim_rooms, dim_date*) using *SQL* and *Excel*.  
2. Built an interactive dashboard in *Power BI* to track key performance indicators (KPIs) such as revenue, occupancy %, bookings, cancellations, and no-show %.  
3. Created comparative analysis across properties, room classes, states, and booking platforms.  
4. Incorporated time-based trends to understand weekday vs. weekend occupancy and cancellations.

## Results & Business Recommendation

The analysis and dashboards provided clear insights into hotel performance:  

- Certain properties consistently outperform others, driving most of the revenue.  
- Room categories vary in profitability; premium rooms experience higher cancellations.  
- Weekend occupancy is higher, but weekday underutilization impacts total revenue.  
- Some booking platforms generate high revenue but also higher cancellation rates.
